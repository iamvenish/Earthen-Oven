A Celebration of North Indian Culinary Heritage

Nestled in the heart of Ahmedabad at Fortune Landmark, Earthen Oven brings you the royal flavours of North India. Our culinary philosophy is rooted in traditional clay oven preparation, capturing the authentic tastes that have defined Indian fine dining for generations.

From the aromatic spices roasted to perfection, to the rich and creamy curries simmered for hours, every dish is a handcrafted masterpiece. Complementing our exquisite menu is a premium rooftop ambiance complete with live music, creating a dining experience that engages all your senses.
